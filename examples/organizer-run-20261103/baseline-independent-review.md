## Limits

No app-arm response, end-to-end human timing, user actions or screenshots were examined here. Four successful calls do not prove general reliability or that either workflow outperforms the other. Compare the later app arm only after its complete captured results are independently reviewed, preserving any failures, context or tool asymmetry and the common fictional data basis.
