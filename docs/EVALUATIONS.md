# Historical evaluation records

The retained protocols and results record tests of earlier development snapshots under the project's former name, ReMatter. They are historical evidence, not results for every later version. The frozen protocol text remains unchanged. For two protocols, the source export adds a historical notice and adapts their verifiers to check that exact notice before hashing the original text.

The [organizer results](ORGANIZER_EVALUATION_RESULTS_20261103.md) describe one synthetic case and its limits.
