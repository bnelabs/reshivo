# Planning-only and intake follow-up — 3 November 2026

The frozen UI journey remains unchanged: intake was cut short at the 4,096-token limit and, after manual entry, a later answer gave a physical addition instruction despite an explicit no-handling constraint. The original UI observations (not included in this export) are the outcome of that run. These follow-up calls are separate implementation checks, not a replacement evaluation.

The intake now requests up to 8,192 completion tokens and allows a 90-second request timeout. It still makes one call and rejects incomplete output; no automatic retry was added. The higher limit can increase cost and wait time. A bounded local phrase check applies when the user explicitly says planning only, no physical handling, or no physical work. It turns the observed direct numerical addition into a proposed planning quantity, replaces other recognized direct material actions with a planning step, and marks locally adapted advice in the visible provenance and saved notes. Digital actions such as adding a synthetic reading remain available. The rule does not equate “no processing equipment” or “no physical testing” with a blanket ban on handling. Physical-action imperatives in trial target or property fields are rejected rather than changing those fields. These checks cover selected English constructions only: other physical instructions or contexts can be missed, and valid text can be changed. They are not a general safety or instruction-following guarantee.
