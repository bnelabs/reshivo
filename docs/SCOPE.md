# Reshivo — product and calculation scope

Reshivo helps a user plan a product when recycled inputs vary. The user describes the material, intended product, property targets and available process. The assistant can propose material, process and design options, ask for missing details and help plan a trial. Suggestions are conditional ideas, not tested formulations.

The project keeps its context through **Options, Trials, Checks and Costs**. Users compare the readings they enter against the limits they choose, and review manufacturing costs for defined setups. A new reading can change that comparison. Saved projects retain the recorded information; saved calculations need review after reopening.

**Adjust a mix** calculates an addition to reach a chosen composition range from an entered portion, input-fraction range and addition stock. The calculation assumes representative fractions and no losses or reactions. It predicts composition under those assumptions, not the performance of a finished product.

Other optional tools use their own stated inputs. An entered density of a processed mix and matching volume can support a mass simulation. A weighed assembly can support a mass adjustment. Neither establishes strength, sound performance or future batch consistency. Repeated readings are not automatically separate batches; published studies remain separate from the user's material.

The app retains older source-data comparisons and support-member calculations as optional tools and software checks. They do not establish a property of another material or qualify a finished product.
