# Source data attribution

The source archive and its metadata originate from:

Kim Hao Ng, Leonard D. Süss, Franziska Hirschel, Michael Müller, Bianca Ricarda Schell, Elke Metzsch-Zilligen, Rudolf Pfaendner and Nico Bruns. **Evaluating the Recyclability and Restabilization of Laboratory Plastics Consumables: Impact of Autoclaving and Mechanical Recycling on Polypropylene**, Zenodo, version 2, record 19370161 (2026).

- Dataset: https://zenodo.org/records/19370161
- Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19370161
- Associated paper: https://doi.org/10.1039/D6SU00069J
- Dataset license in repository metadata: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`raw/Tensile_tests.zip` and `raw/zenodo-19370161.json` are retained source copies. `raw/pp_tensile_report_summary.csv` is a derivative extraction of reported mean/SD values from the 18 PDF test reports. It does not add new experiments, infer additive-code mappings or claim batch-level uncertainty. Source member filenames remain traceable. All source-file SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `sources.json`.
